ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with an environmental purpose.

EcoMind AI is a prototype intelligent environment monitoring and sustainability recommendation system.

Problem statement

Environmental readings are often collected in separate places and are difficult to translate into practical actions. EcoMind provides one place to record indicators and receive understandable recommendations.

Project objectives

  • Record environmental parameters.
  • Present observations in a dashboard.
  • Apply generative AI to explain readings.
  • Suggest measurable sustainability actions.

System modules

01 · Data input

Manual entry of environmental observations.

02 · Data storage

SQLite database for readings and history.

03 · Visualization

Dashboard metrics and air-quality trend chart.

04 · AI recommendation

Gemini API integration with local fallback suggestions.
